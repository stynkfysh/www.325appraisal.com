What every report includes

  • A USPAP-compliant opinion of market value
  • The effective date you need — current or past
  • Selected comparable sales with explained adjustments
  • Neighborhood and market-conditions analysis
  • A description of the property and its condition
  • The appraiser's signed certification
  • A PDF delivered by email
  • Answers to reasonable follow-up questions

How fees are set

Three things move a fee: how unusual the property is compared with recent sales, how much research the effective date requires (older retrospective dates take longer), and the level of responsibility tied to the intended use — reports prepared for litigation or tax filings carry more of it. Large acreage, very high-value homes and properties far from the nearest sales are quoted individually.

Payment

After you approve the written quote, you receive a secure online invoice. The assignment is scheduled once it is paid.

GroundWork Report — $99 market data report

Not ready for an appraisal? The GroundWork Report, offered through our parent site, lays out every qualifying nearby sale, the county record for the parcel and twelve months of market trends. It is a data report, not an appraisal or an opinion of value, and the $99 is credited toward a Desktop or Standard appraisal. Learn about the GroundWork Report on brianward.com.
