Privacy

Privacy notice

Effective November 13, 2026

This notice explains how 325 Appraisal, operated by Brian Ward, handles information collected through www.325appraisal.com.

Information we collect

When you submit the request form or email us, we receive the details you choose to provide — such as your name, email address, phone number, the property address, the purpose of the appraisal, relevant dates and your message. Our hosting provider also processes standard technical data (for example IP address and browser type) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse, including through a bot-detection check on the request form.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request, prepare a quote and, if you engage us, complete the appraisal.
  • To send a confirmation of your request and related correspondence.
  • To protect the site and the request form from spam and misuse.
  • To meet professional record-keeping obligations that apply to appraisers.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that host the website and deliver email on our behalf, with the intended users of an appraisal you authorize, or when required by law, subpoena or professional regulation. Appraisal work files are kept confidential as required by professional standards.

Cookies and tracking

The site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The bot-detection service on the request form may set cookies or similar technical data needed for that check. Because we do not track visitors across other websites, the site does not change its behavior in response to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

Retention and security

Inquiries are kept as long as needed to respond and for reasonable business records; appraisal files are retained for the periods required of licensed appraisers. Information is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Your choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information you submitted, subject to records we must keep by law or professional standards. Email contact@325appraisal.com.

Changes

We may update this notice; the effective date above shows when it last changed.
