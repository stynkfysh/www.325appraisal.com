What we appraise for

Valuations for legal, tax and family decisions

Every assignment is a non-lender appraisal: the report is written for you, your attorney, your CPA or the court — not for a bank.

Date-of-Death Appraisals

Retrospective values fixed to the day of passing, for step-up in basis, estate returns, probate and trust administration.

Estate & Trust Appraisals

Independent values for probate inventories, trust funding and distributions, and buyouts among beneficiaries.

Divorce Appraisals

Neutral valuations of the family home for settlement, mediation or trial, with retrospective dates when the case calls for them.

Bankruptcy Appraisals

Supported market values for Chapter 7, 11 and 13 schedules, exemption planning and lien questions.

Details →

Proposition 19 Appraisals

Market values for parent-to-child and grandparent transfers, so families can see how a reassessment under Prop 19 will play out.

Details →

Property Tax & Gift Tax Appraisals

Evidence for assessment appeals and decline-in-value reviews, plus values for gifts of real estate reported to the IRS.

Details →

Pre-Purchase Appraisals

An independent value before you commit — especially for cash, off-market, unusual or family purchases with no lender appraisal.

Pre-Sale Appraisals

A defensible list price before you go to market, whether you sell with an agent, on your own, or to a family member.

Family Transfer Appraisals

Fair market value for sales, gifts and buyouts between relatives, documented for the IRS and for the rest of the family.

Details →

Insurance Dispute Appraisals

Market-value evidence for claims and coverage disputes, including pre-loss and post-loss values and diminution in value.

PMI Removal Appraisals
