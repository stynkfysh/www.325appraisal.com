The San Bernardino County housing landscape

Redlands and Upland grew up as citrus towns, and their older neighborhoods keep Victorian, Craftsman and Spanish Revival homes on tree-lined streets. Former dairies and vineyards in Chino, Ontario, Fontana and the Etiwanda area of Rancho Cucamonga have given way to master-planned developments such as Ontario Ranch and The Preserve, with newer homes on smaller lots. In Victorville and Hesperia, newer subdivisions sit beside older homes on half-acre and larger parcels, and many buyers come up from the valley looking for more land. Warehouse and logistics construction along the Interstate 10 and Interstate 15 corridors has changed the surroundings of some established neighborhoods in Fontana, Ontario and Rialto.

Valuation considerations

Mello-Roos special taxes in Ontario Ranch, The Preserve, Etiwanda and newer Fontana tracts vary from one subdivision to the next, so comparable sales should be matched on tax burden as well as size and age. High Desert parcels raise their own questions about septic systems, private wells, unpaved access and manufactured homes, and foothill neighborhoods in Rancho Cucamonga, Upland and Yucaipa face wildfire hazard and insurance constraints. Nearby distribution centers, rail lines and the Ontario International Airport flight path are location factors that can separate otherwise similar homes.

Is a desktop appraisal the right fit?

For a Fontana, Chino Hills or Rancho Cucamonga tract home with frequent same-plan sales, a desktop appraisal is typically all an estate, trust or divorce file requires. Hesperia properties with outbuildings or manufactured homes, desert acreage, and mountain cabins of uncertain condition are a different matter and benefit from a site visit.
