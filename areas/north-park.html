North Park Home Appraisals

Independent appraisals for estates, trusts, divorce, bankruptcy, taxes and litigation — in person or by desktop.

East of Balboa Park and west of Interstate 805, North Park grew in the early 1900s as a streetcar suburb, and its commercial heart remains the corner of 30th Street and University Avenue beneath the North Park sign. Bungalow-lined side streets, designated historic districts and a dense mix of small apartment buildings make it a place where older houses and urban amenities overlap.

The North Park housing market

Craftsman bungalows and bungalow courts from the 1910s and 1920s give way to Spanish Eclectic and Minimal Traditional homes built through the 1930s and early 1940s, followed by postwar ranch houses and two-story stucco apartment buildings. Lots are generally narrow and many have alley access, which has made the neighborhood a frequent setting for garage conversions and new companion units. Detached homes come up in small numbers and draw remodelers and owner-occupants alike, while condominiums and townhomes along University Avenue, El Cajon Boulevard and Park Boulevard offer the more common entry point. Newer mid-rise residential buildings near the commercial corridors keep adding to that attached-housing supply.

What shapes value in North Park

Designated historic districts such as Burlingame, North Park Dryden and Shirley Ann Place bring design review and the possibility of Mills Act contracts, both of which bear on value and on future property taxes. Because so many lots now carry a converted garage or a newer companion unit, separating permitted from unpermitted living area and supporting any contribution from rental income are routine steps. Homes closest to the busiest commercial blocks gain walkability but also more noise and parking competition, which buyers weigh differently.

Around North Park

Morley Field's sports complex, tennis courts and disc golf course sit at the neighborhood's edge in Balboa Park, and the restored North Park Theatre, the 1912 Stevens Building and the shops of 30th Street and University Avenue form its commercial core.
