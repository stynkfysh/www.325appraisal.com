Imperial Beach occupies the southern reach of the San Diego County coast, bordered by San Diego Bay, the Tijuana River estuary and the Pacific, with the Silver Strand running north to Coronado. Highly residential and mostly renter-occupied, it offers beach cottages, duplexes and condominiums on a compact grid within reach of the pier.

The Imperial Beach housing market

Early beach cottages, including some moved from Coronado's Tent City in the 1930s, set the original scale, and post-war homes, duplexes and apartment and condominium buildings filled in around them. The Seacoast area around the pier and the Seaview district along Seacoast Drive south of Imperial Beach Boulevard hold most oceanfront homes and condominiums, while the Bayview district north of State Route 75 and inland neighborhoods such as Oneonta and Mar Vista contain smaller single-family homes and multifamily buildings. Buyers include Navy households from nearby bases, first-time purchasers seeking a beach address and investors focused on rental income. Mixed-use zoning along Palm Avenue and Seacoast Drive allows newer condominium and commercial projects.

What shapes value in Imperial Beach

Cross-border sewage flows from the Tijuana River have kept local beaches closed for long stretches and affected air quality, an external factor that buyers weigh alongside the beach location itself. Helicopter training at the Navy's outlying landing field produces noise in the southern neighborhoods, and low-lying blocks near the bay, the estuary and the oceanfront face flood and sea-level-rise exposure. With many rentals and small multifamily properties, rental income, legal unit counts and ADU permits are frequent appraisal questions.

Around Imperial Beach

The Imperial Beach Pier and Pier Plaza, the Tijuana River National Estuarine Research Reserve, Border Field State Park and the Bayshore Bikeway frame daily life, and Silver Strand State Beach lies just to the north. Naval Outlying Landing Field Imperial Beach and the Silver Strand Training Complex keep the Navy a constant presence.
