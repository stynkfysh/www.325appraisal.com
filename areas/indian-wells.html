Indian Wells, a compact and mostly residential city between Palm Desert and La Quinta, keeps most of its homes inside private, gated country clubs against the Santa Rosa foothills. Seasonal owners and retirees drive the market, and the spring professional tennis tournament fills the city each March.

The Indian Wells housing market

Eldorado Country Club and Indian Wells Country Club both date from the 1950s and hold many mid-century homes, while The Vintage Club and The Reserve contain large custom estates on foothill and fairway lots. Toscana Country Club adds newer Mediterranean-style construction, and Desert Horizons offers larger single-family homes where club membership comes with ownership. Condominiums and villas along the fairways give buyers a smaller-scale option within several of the clubs.

What shapes value in Indian Wells

Membership structure is central to value here: clubs differ in whether golf membership is required, optional or bundled with the home, so comparables are matched on that basis before physical adjustments begin. Short-term rentals are tightly restricted, which limits any income-based reasoning. Golf frontage, mountain views, original mid-century condition versus full renovation, and lot size within each club account for most of the remaining differences.

Around Indian Wells

The Indian Wells Tennis Garden hosts the spring tournament, the city-owned Indian Wells Golf Resort offers public play on two courses, and a row of resort hotels lines Highway 111.
