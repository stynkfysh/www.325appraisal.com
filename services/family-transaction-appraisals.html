Family Transfer Appraisals

Fair market value for sales, gifts and buyouts between relatives, documented for the IRS and for the rest of the family.

Selling a home to a son or daughter, buying out a sibling's share, or adding a relative to title all raise the same question: what is the property worth? A third-party appraisal settles it with evidence, which keeps the transaction clean for tax purposes and keeps the peace with family members who are not part of the deal.

Why an outside value helps

When relatives agree on a price among themselves, the IRS may treat any discount below market value as a gift, and other heirs may later question whether the deal was fair. An appraisal records the market value at the time of the transfer, so everyone can see how the price compares.

Partial interests and buyouts

Many family transfers involve one co-owner buying out another. We appraise the whole property; if your attorney or CPA needs a separate analysis of a fractional interest, we will say so up front rather than stretch the assignment beyond its scope.

Property tax considerations

Transfers between parents and children can also trigger reassessment under Proposition 19. If that is part of your planning, see our Prop 19 page and mention it in your request so one report can address both uses.
