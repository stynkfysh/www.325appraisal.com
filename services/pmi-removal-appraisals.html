PMI Removal Appraisals

A current value showing the equity you have gained, for servicers that accept a borrower-ordered appraisal.

If your home has gained value since you bought it, your loan may now be below the loan-to-value level at which private mortgage insurance can be dropped. Removing it can save a meaningful amount every month, and the request usually depends on an appraisal that shows today's market value.

Call your servicer first

Servicers set their own rules for borrower-requested PMI cancellation, including how long you must have owned the home, the loan-to-value they require, and who may perform the appraisal. Some order the appraisal themselves or use an approved list; others accept a report ordered by the borrower. Ask before you order so the report you pay for is one they will accept.

What the report shows

When the servicer accepts an independent appraisal, we provide a current market value supported by recent comparable sales. Most servicers expect an interior inspection for this purpose, so PMI removal requests are usually completed as Standard appraisals, which are available throughout San Diego and Riverside Counties.
